# COST — Buffett-inspired business memo

**Verdict: OWN THE BUSINESS** — business quality, not the stock's purchase price.

The economics look strong enough to merit an owner's attention. ROE held above the 15% classroom hurdle in every year, including 28.3% in FY2022 and 25.8% in FY2026. Owner earnings (operating cash flow minus capital spending) rose from $3,501 million in FY2022 to $9,382 million in FY2026. Net income also rose, from $5,844 million to $9,226 million. All 3/3 Buffett checks pass.

Cash generation and consistent returns make a compelling business-quality case, but the numbers do not themselves establish the durability of Costco's competitive moat or management discipline. The owner's cash-flow proxy also deducts all capital spending rather than just maintenance spending.

**Trust condition:** I trust this as a first-pass business screen, but would independently evaluate moat, management and maintenance capital needs before making any investment decision.
