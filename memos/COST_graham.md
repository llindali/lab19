# COST — Graham-inspired business memo

**Verdict: WATCH** — business quality, not the stock's purchase price.

A defensive investor should not dismiss the balance sheet merely because earnings look attractive. Costco's latest current ratio is 1.06, below the prescribed 2.0 threshold, so the liquidity screen fails. Net income was positive in every fiscal year and rose from $5,844 million in FY2022 to $9,226 million in FY2026. Graham therefore passes 2/3 screens, but not his stricter current-liquidity requirement.

The failed screen is a call for additional examination, not proof of distress. The supplied figures do not establish a bargain purchase price or a margin of safety, so this is a business-quality watch rather than a purchase recommendation.

**Threshold retained: 2.0** — I keep Graham's conservative defensive-liquidity hurdle because lowering it solely to change the verdict would weaken the screen's intended protection.
